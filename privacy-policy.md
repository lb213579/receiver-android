# Privacy Policy
Last updated: 26-July-2026

OpenDroneID is committed to protecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use the application.

1. Information collected:
  * No personal information is collected.
  * Automatically collected data:<BR>
    Any received drone ID signals will by default be logged to a csv file on the file system in your phone.
    This logging can be disabled via the menu in the app.<BR>
    This logged information will (per the nature of Drone ID) contain GPS coordinates of the detected drones and their operators.
    Since the range of reception of drone ID signals is limited, those GSP coordinates will be somewhere in the vicinity of the location of the phone, upon which the OpenDroneID application is running.<BR>
    None of the logged data will ever leave the phone upon which the app is running, unless the user themselves copies the stored csv files somewhere.

  * Permissions:<BR>
    The application requires the following permissions:
    - Bluetooth
    - WiFi
    - GPS / Location
  
2. How We Use Your Information:<BR>
No information is collected from by the developers of this app.

3. Children’s Privacy:<BR>
Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from anyone under the age of 13.

4. Changes to This Policy:<BR>
We may update this privacy policy from time to time.
Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
